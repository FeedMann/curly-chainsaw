Your controls, before you jump in

When loading finishes, choose PLAY TEST ARENA. Click the canvas or press Tab to capture the mouse; Esc releases it.

Spawn & inspect

  • M open / close the spawn menu. Aim first, then choose a prop or physics body.
  • F3 spawn entcrel · F7 spawn a melon target.
  • L mark where you look: an orange debug cross for 2 seconds. Coordinates, normal and distance print to the console.
  • E use · LMB on a held pistol or socket to drop / spawn its item.
  • B collision AABBs · F2 floor grid · F6 clear registry entities.

Move & fight

  • W A S D move · mouse / arrow keys look.
  • Space jump · Shift sprint · Ctrl walk.
  • LMB fire · RMB secondary fire / AWP scope. Reload is automatic on empty.
  • Hold Q for the weapon wheel, release to equip. Mouse wheel switches weapons.
  • F4 refill ammo · F9 reload map · F11 stats.

` developer console · F1 all keybinds. Console commands: lookpos [range], phclear (loose physics props), menu (main menu).
